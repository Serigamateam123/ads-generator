FRAMEWORK: US VS THEM
The ad creates a stark binary: the old/wrong/generic option vs YOUR product as the
obvious winner. It works by (a) validating the customer's existing frustration with
other products, and (b) letting the comparison do the selling so the brand never
sounds boastful.

WHY IT WORKS PSYCHOLOGICALLY
- Contrast effect: presenting a weak left column makes the right column look
  dramatically superior even if the wins are small
- Cognitive fluency: bullet-point checklists are fast to read; red ✗ vs green ✓
  is processed in milliseconds before the brain even reads the text
- Loss aversion: naming competitor problems first triggers fear of making a
  "wrong" purchase — which then drives action toward the safe/verified option
- "Don't Buy This Pill" variant uses reverse psychology — the headline is a dare,
  which increases curiosity and creates a pattern interrupt in a scroll feed

WHAT TO KEEP
- Clean two-column split: left = generic problem, right = Serigama solution
- Red ✗ / green ✓ visual system — universal, no language barrier (good for
  bilingual MY market)
- Bold all-caps section label "US VS THEM" at top as category anchor
- Minimal background — lets the product bottle be the only hero on the right side
- Strong black bar at the bottom for a punchy closer claim
